Why 4 People?

1:1 networking coffee chats can feel like a high-pressure job interview. 50-person mixers are exhausting. Connect Four is the ultimate sweet spot. It’s a casual, self-organized lunch where the conversation flows naturally, everyone gets to speak, and real relationships actually stick.
